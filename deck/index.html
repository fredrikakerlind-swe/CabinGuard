01 / Background

A house can stand empty for months.

The problem

Summer houses stand empty all winter, mountain cabins between visits. Leaks and frost can go unnoticed until the owner returns.

The opportunity

An empty property has a relatively stable acoustic baseline. Changes may reveal an emerging problem.

Why Haysquare?

Reuse our connected‑device expertise: telemetry, anomaly detection, event processing and customer‑facing services.

Start with water damage. Treat intrusion detection as an additional benefit.

Source: Summer House Surveillance, 25 Nov 2026, pp. 1, 4–5

05 / The idea

One device. Broader protection.

01

Listen

Low‑power microphone plus temperature and humidity.

Understand

No audio recordings. No router. No installation. A time‑stamped event history could also help insurers see when an incident began.

06 / Product requirements

Designed to be left alone all winter.

Effortless setup

Switch on, scan a QR code, place the device. No electrician, plumber or Wi‑Fi setup.

Ultra‑low power

6 to 12 months on replaceable batteries. LTE‑M, event‑driven transmission and sparse heartbeats.

Reliable detection

Always‑on low‑power sensing, water first. Temperature and humidity. Few false alarms; works in the cold.

Privacy & affordability

On‑device audio analysis; no raw recordings by default. Hardware cost below SEK 350 at volume.

Initial targets

Setup < 2 min

Average power ≤ 2 mW

Operating temp −20 to +50 °C

Proposed requirements from design discussions; targets, not validated results

08 / PoC · Success criteria

What does success look like?

≥90%

Detection rate

For defined audible water scenarios, with distance and door state recorded.

≤2 min

Alert latency

From sustained water sound to alert, when connected.

≤2 mW

Average power

Whole‑system target including radio, measured or extrapolated transparently.

Longer‑term target: ≤1 false alarm per house per month. Compare against simple leak sensors and measure incremental coverage and missed events. A short PoC cannot establish a monthly false‑alarm rate with confidence.

Proposed test criteria; longer field validation required for reliability claims

10 / Beyond the cabin

Same core. Many empty places.

How we found them: we reduced CabinGuard to its core, understanding what happens where nobody is, using sensors that are already there, and asked where else that holds.

Unattended places

Telecom sites and substations, clubhouses and churches, schools after hours, vacant premises.

Silent failures

Cold and freezer rooms, machine and fan rooms (pumps, fans, compressors), boats in harbour.

People and animals

Older people living at home (daily power patterns), dogs home alone, livestock barns.

Land and wildlife

Wild boar on farmland and golf courses: detect, deter with unpredictable sounds, alert hunters.

Close to vehicles

Tool theft from tradespeople’s vans; sound diagnostics via the Dina Mil app.

Signals we reuse

Sound, the electricity meter’s HAN port, temperature and humidity, and devices already in place.

Haydays workshop discussion, 25 Nov 2026
